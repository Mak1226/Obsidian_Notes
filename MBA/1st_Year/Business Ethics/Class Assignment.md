
# Governing Responsibility (Governance)

### Exercise power with integrity, transparency and accountability in decision making and oversight

## Framework

- Find recurring journey
- **What threatens this relationship?**
- What values do we care about not compromising?
- What helps this relationship flourish?
- Where does it drift?
- What future stewardship will this relationship need?

### Find recurring journey
- Strategic decision-making
- Stakeholder communication
- policy formulation and updates
- approval of budgets and investment
- continuous ethical improvement
- risk and compliance oversight
- sustainability oversight
- performance and accountability review
- innovation with responsibility
- vision to value creation

### What threatens this relationship?
- stakeholder priority
- prioritize profit
- short term
- compliance
- social gravity
- blindness to externality
- rigid mental model
- diffusion of responsibility
