# The Hidden Org Chart: Redesigning Roles at LSV (Quick Case) — Notes

📄 Case PDF: [[Hidden_Org_Chart_Redesigning_Roles_at_LSV.pdf]]

## Quick Read
> HBP Quick Case (fictitious). **Shobesh Patel** is five weeks into the role of **Service Head for LSV India's operations team**. LSV Inc. (Sunnyvale, CA; customized pharma-ERP software; founded 2013) runs 70% of its global delivery through **LSV India** (est. 2016), which now has project overruns and an **18% rework rate** (vs. a 10% benchmark and LSV's own 9% norm). CEO Prashant Gupta has ordered a **60% operational-efficiency improvement by January 1** — cutting average project cycle time from 120 to 75 days and raising billable utilization from 58% to 83%. LSV India staffs every project with a rigid **2 consultants + 1 lead consultant (LC)** formula regardless of complexity, and several long-serving LCs are technically outdated but have **direct, informal access to the CEO**. Shobesh must fix the structure while navigating that hidden power network. The Ask: what should Shobesh do?

## Case Notes

### Key facts
- **The mandate:** +60% efficiency by Jan 1 = project cycle time **120 → 75 days**, billable utilization **58% → 83%**; rework must come down from **18%** toward LSV's 9% norm.
- **The trigger:** the Truvance Pharma upgrade ran **4,200 hours vs. a 3,000-hour budget**, two weeks late, cut margins, hit morale, and triggered a **contract penalty**; services revenue is **down 8% YoY — the first drop ever** for LSV India, from lost extensions + penalties.
- **The rigid structure:** fixed **2+1** teams "to simplify staffing" — ignores project complexity. On Truvance a strong consultant fixed defects at night while the assigned LC struggled with basic integration scripts; on a routine support job two skilled consultants sat idle.
- **The hidden org chart:** consultants (e.g., Anjali Singh) informally lead projects and are the client's real point of contact; some LCs were **promoted when the unit was tiny and haven't kept pace technically** (Priya Sharma); certain LCs **email the CEO directly** and two attended a family wedding with him in Bengaluru.
- **Shobesh's position:** formal authority to reshuffle teams and is the intermediary between LCs and the CEO, but "the maze of tight informal relationships" makes him unsure how much to do unilaterally.
- **The buzz:** Priya — swapping LC roles is sensitive because of CEO ties. Anjali — informal leadership should be formally recognized. **Jacob Ferrera (CIO, Truvance)** — "we never see the right person in the right seat"; considering other vendors. **Anamta Mir (Arvenza Therapeutics)** — demo crashed twice at kickoff; postponing their analytics module. **Ashwin Patel (LC)** — "changing team structure means stepping on toes… loyalty matters as much as efficiency."

### Central decision
- **What should Shobesh do** to redesign LSV India's team structure and role assignments — aligning skills with project complexity and hitting the 60% efficiency mandate — while managing the informal power network of long-serving, CEO-connected lead consultants and protecting at-risk client relationships?

### Key takeaways
- **Formal chart vs. real network:** authority sits with LCs, but the technical leadership, client relationships and influence flow through a different set of people — diagnose the "hidden org chart" (real leaders, brokers, bottlenecks, CEO back-channels) before restructuring.
- **Match staffing to work:** replace the one-size 2+1 formula with flexible team size and skill mix by project complexity; formalize strong consultants as project leads where they already lead in practice.
- **Manage the politics, don't ignore it:** use transparent, performance-based criteria; align the CEO on principles up front so informal channels can't quietly reverse changes; offer displaced LCs re-skilling, client-relationship or delivery-assurance roles rather than blunt demotion; pilot on a few projects and show results.
- **Risks of formalizing the informal:** demotivating displaced LCs, CEO-channel resistance, and disrupting live client engagements — sequence carefully and protect client continuity (Truvance, Arvenza).
- **Metrics need the structural fix + QA discipline:** utilization and cycle-time targets won't hold just by squeezing people; the rework rate points to a skills/role mismatch that has to be solved at the source.
