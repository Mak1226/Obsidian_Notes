# Lecture 1 — Value of the Firm & the Principal–Agent Problem

📄 Slides: [[Lecture_1_slides.pdf]] · *This is a pre-read map — study the deck for detail.*

## The two ideas
1. **What should a firm maximise?** → the **value of the firm** (shareholder wealth), not profit / sales / survival.
2. **Why value leaks in practice** → the **principal–agent (agency) problem**: managers act for owners but have their own incentives.

---

## 1. Goal of financial management
- Slide lists 7 candidate goals (survive, avoid distress, beat rivals, max sales/share, min costs, max profits, steady EPS growth).
- All are flawed proxies — "profit" ignores **timing, risk, and investment needed**.
- **Right goal: maximise long-run firm value** = PV of expected future cash flows, risk-adjusted.

## 2. Accounting vs Finance
- **Accounting** = record/report the **past**; accuracy & compliance; GAAP/IFRS, ratios.
- **Finance** = decide the **future**; forecasting, valuation, value creation; NPV, IRR, CAPM.
- Deck compares them on 6 fronts: definition, focus, key activities, users, time horizon, tools.
- Key point: decisions run on **cash flows**, not accounting profit.

## 3. Where corporate finance creates vs destroys value
Deck has a table across 7 levers. Pattern:

| Lever | Creates value | Destroys value |
|---|---|---|
| Investment | Positive NPV | Negative NPV / misallocation |
| Financing | Optimal debt–equity, lower WACC | Over-leverage / dilution |
| Dividends | Balanced payout + reinvest | Overpay or hoard cash |
| Risk mgmt | Hedging stabilises flows | Risk ignored / over-hedged |
| Working capital | Efficient liquidity | Idle funds |
| Governance | Managers aligned with owners | Agency costs, fraud |
| Strategy | Smart M&A | Overpay, bad integration |

## 4. Principal–Agent across ownership types
Deck compares **Government / Private / Family-owned** on ownership, capital, objectives, governance, transparency, efficiency, risk-taking, succession.
- **Govt/PSU:** welfare + profit, bureaucratic, slow, high transparency.
- **Private:** profit + growth, professional, fast, moderate transparency.
- **Family-owned:** legacy + sustainability, family-driven, conservative, low transparency.

## 5. The agency problem
- **Agent** = manager (acts). **Principal** = owner/shareholder (gets the benefits).
- Problem: managers make decisions that **benefit themselves, not owners**.
- Driven by **separation of ownership & control** + **information asymmetry**.

## 6. Principal–agent conflicts (know these 4)
- **Reduced effort** (shirking)
- **Excessive perk consumption**
- **Entrenchment** — making yourself hard to replace *(entrenchment = a habit/system set so firmly it's very hard to change)*
- **Risk avoidance** — skipping good but risky projects to protect your job

## 7. How organisational structure mitigates agency problems
Six buckets in the deck:
1. **Governance** — independent board; audit/comp/risk committees; split CEO & Chairman.
2. **Incentive alignment** — pay tied to long-term performance (stock/options); **clawback** clauses *(return of pay already given)*.
3. **Transparency & accountability** — internal controls, audits, whistleblower channels.
4. **Decentralisation** — profit centres, unit-level accountability, cross-functional teams.
5. **Ownership** — managerial shareholding, ESOPs.
6. **Monitoring & control** — internal audit, active (institutional) shareholders, external checks: managerial labour market, credit-rating agencies.

---

## Glossary to walk in with
- **Firm value / shareholder wealth** — market value of owners' equity; the objective function.
- **WACC** — blended cost of debt + equity; financing aims to minimise it.
- **NPV / IRR / CAPM** — core finance tools (covered later).
- **Agency cost** — value lost to the manager–owner conflict (monitoring + bonding + residual loss).
- **Entrenchment**, **Clawback** — see above.
